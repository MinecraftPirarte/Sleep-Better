Why cutting sleep short is worse than it seems

When you cut a night short by 1–2 hours, you mostly lose sleep from the end of the night — which contains the most REM. A 6-hour night doesn't give you 75% of an 8-hour night's benefit. It may give you less than 50% of the REM sleep, which is the stage most directly tied to academic performance, emotional regulation, and memory.
